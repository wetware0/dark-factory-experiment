# Dark Factory PAVE Research

Use both knowledge services deliberately:

- `wtgkb`: current WTG product/process/codebase knowledge relevant to the PAVE task.
- `sbkb`: durable second-brain notes and prior learnings relevant to the task type, artifact class, and repositories.

Capture:

1. WTG knowledge queries and citations.
2. Second Brain queries and citations.
3. WTG.AI.Prompts skills selected for the next phases.
4. Any knowledge gaps that require human follow-up.

Do not write new learnings to sbkb in this command. Writeback belongs to the dedicated self-learning PAVE task.
