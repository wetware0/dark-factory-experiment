# Dark Factory PAVE Critic

Run a critic pass as a normal workflow node.

The critic must assess:

1. Requirement fit against the PAVE work item or incident.
2. Correct use of WTG knowledge and second-brain context.
3. Cross-repo consistency for modular CargoWise changes.
4. Build/test evidence and any validation gaps.
5. Risk of generated artifacts being misleading or incomplete.
6. Whether the eDoc evidence report can truthfully include the dashboard log and critic output.

Record critic status, summary, score if used, and structured findings in the factory portal.
